# Response playbooks

Confirmed direction: extend the selected Response desk identity in dark pink/purple with light mode. Existing Satoshi typography, semantic tokens, normal links, native controls, and evidence boundaries remain authoritative. No visual identity replacement or new illustration is needed for this operational flow.

Public `/playbooks` and detail pages are Read surfaces: choose guidance, understand applicability, follow response phases, inspect sources, and start a private record. Private project responses are Operate surfaces: answer, record actions/evidence, save, and revisit history. Avoid scorecards and automatic “safe” outcomes.

Mobbin inspected [incident.io post-incident flow](https://mobbin.com/screens/06523c4e-18ca-452b-8b4c-d89c9feb5daa): compact task rows grouped under named phases keep action and completion together. Wisesama adapts that task hierarchy while using a project-owned response and an explicit save, without borrowing assignment/team permissions the product does not have. The Revolut lost-device result was consumer-specific and not used. The Searchable setup result was not an incident response reference.

Private composition: project backlink, incident title and source version, scope, applicability questions, grouped response tasks, summary/evidence/status, save state; adjacent sources/history on wide screens and in reading order on smaller screens. Public composition reuses the established guide article and task list, with no interactive checkboxes pretending to save progress. Empty/unstarted, loading, failed read, failed save, conflict/reload, saved, attested closure, and reopened records are concrete states.

Quality bar: unknown history stays unknown; version/platform/channel/window all visible for the vendor incident; closure cannot imply device verification; raw credentials/logs discouraged; draft remains after failed save; conflict cannot overwrite a newer record; actual evidence/time/commit and self-reported context remain separate. Parent captures desktop/mobile/light in one bounded round and supplies a fresh independent finish review.
