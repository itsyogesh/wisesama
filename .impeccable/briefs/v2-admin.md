Target: `/admin` and its user, advisory, source, subscription, failed-job, and audit routes in `apps/intel/src/components/admin`.

Mode: Operate. An authorized platform operator finds a real record, inspects its evidence and consequences, makes one scoped change, and verifies the persisted result. The existing Response desk identity, Satoshi, charcoal/purple palette, light theme, and explicit source/customer-response boundaries remain fixed. This is a researched operational extension, not an identity replacement or a new visual-choice tournament.

The user specifically requested Mobbin research for every flow. `docs/ADMIN-DESIGN.md` records inspected references and accepted/rejected patterns separately for users, advisories, sources, subscriptions, and failed jobs, including access deactivation, source pause, consent suppression, and retry confirmation transitions.

Composition: separate operator shell/navigation and live access gate; compact searchable lists with persistent identity/status; full record pages with authoritative facts beside a scoped reason/confirmation form; recorded outcome and recent audit; dedicated durable job state and attempt history. Overview leads to actual work requiring attention instead of decorative metrics or charts.

Quality bar: no cached privilege grant, no Dotsama role reuse, no signup/impersonation/delete controls, no invented successful source health, no direct upstream advisory fact editing, no forced consent, no replay of sent mail, and no successful-job claim inferred from HTTP200. Revision errors preserve entered work. New records and older diagnostic limits stay explicit. Identifiers, source times, errors, and action effects remain inspectable in dark/light desktop/mobile layouts. All operational imagery is real UI content; no new raster is needed.

Source contracts are `docs/ADMIN-API-CONTRACT.md` and `docs/ADMIN-OPS.md`. Parent integration owns real API/storage/browser evidence and the fresh independent finish review after the bounded capture pass. Child frontend checks are not a production-readiness or security certification.
